# FAQ

In-app: [`/help/faq`](/help/faq)

## What property types does Easy Rental support?

Warehouse / industrial bays, commercial shops and offices, and residential homes (apartments, villas, PG/rooms). Filter by segment on Listings.

## How do deposits work?

Residential (and many commercial) units store a deposit in months of rent. On approval the ledger posts a separate deposit line. Typical residential range is 2–10 months.

## Is GST charged on rent?

Warehouse and commercial approvals post 18% GST on rent in this product. Residential approvals do not post GST by default (confirm with a CA — treatment depends on use and registration).

## How does KYC work? Do you store Aadhaar?

No Aadhaar images or numbers are stored. The demo uses a licensed-provider reference. Production will redirect to IDfy / Signzy / HyperVerge (or similar) and keep only status + reference ids.

## Can I cancel an application?

Landlords can deny open applications. Occupants should contact the landlord to end a lease; formal cancellation / notice workflows are not fully automated yet.

## I am an NRI — can I see USD/CAD/GBP?

Yes as a display preference on the landlord desk. The charge currency remains INR. Conversion uses a labelled demo FX table, not a live feed.

## What does pricing look like?

See pricing when published. Planned shape: Free plan with 1 unit, then per-unit paid plans billed in INR + 18% GST. Billing provider is not live yet.

## Is this the live EasyRent launch?

This build is launch-ready scaffolding. Demo data is labelled **DEMO ·** and isolated from real signups. Live payments, SMS OTP, and licensed KYC still need production wiring.
