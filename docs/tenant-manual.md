# Tenant / applicant user manual

In-app: [`/help/tenant`](/help/tenant)

## Overview

Applicants browse public listings, apply for a unit, complete demo KYC, and (after approval) pay the demo ledger. Live bank collection and licensed KYC are not enabled yet.

## Steps

1. Browse [`/listings`](/listings) and filter by segment (warehouse, commercial, residential).
2. Open a unit → Apply. Warehouse/commercial forms ask for company; residential treats you as a household.
3. Complete demo KYC at [`/account/verify`](/account/verify) — provider reference only (**no Aadhaar images**).
4. After approval, use [`/account/payments`](/account/payments) (demo adapter only until live billing).
5. Optional shop for approved occupants at [`/shop`](/shop).

## Privacy

Review [`/legal/privacy`](/legal/privacy). From Account, use **Download my data** or **Request erasure** (redacts personal fields; ledger history may remain for the landlord).

## Pricing

See pricing when published. Platform billing for landlords is separate from your rent to the landlord.
